Architecting the Confluence of Cognitive Computing & Autonomous Agents

The Computational and Cognitive Intelligence Club (CCIC) serves as an advanced collaborative ecosystem anchored in Sairam's CSE (AIML) Department. We bridge computational neuroscience, large-scale multi-modal foundation models, and embedded cognitive edge systems to solve acute engineering challenges.
